# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, argue about numbers and evidence rather than people, and assume good faith.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer through the address on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sparkz400). Reports are handled confidentially.
